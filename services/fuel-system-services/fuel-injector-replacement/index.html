Fuel Injector Replacement

Fuel injectors meter fuel into the engine according to electronic control commands. Replacement addresses an injector that is confirmed to leak, malfunction electrically, or deliver incorrectly in a way that cannot be suitably corrected. Gasoline port injection, direct injection, and diesel systems have different parts and service procedures.

D&S / SERVICE DEPT.08.02

Signs that warrant diagnosis

  • A cylinder misfires, starting becomes difficult, or fuel-related operating data is abnormal.
  • Testing identifies leakage or a delivery imbalance associated with a specific injector.

Avoid replacing by code alone

Ignition faults, compression problems, wiring damage, and fuel-pressure issues can imitate an injector failure. Deposits may affect some injectors, but cleaning is not a repair for a broken electrical winding or physical damage. The fuel source and any contamination should also be considered.

What replacement can include

The affected injector and circuit are evaluated before the correct component and sealing parts are selected. High-pressure connections and any single-use parts follow the applicable procedure. Certain systems require coding, calibration, or adaptation after installation. Leak checks and evaluation of the original running complaint help verify the result.

Before your visit

Describe whether symptoms occur cold, hot, or after refueling, and bring previous diagnostic findings. A flashing check engine light with severe roughness or a strong fuel smell calls for prompt guidance. Stop safely if fuel leakage is suspected rather than continuing to run the engine to reproduce the complaint.

Fuel Injector Cleaning

Fuel injector cleaning targets deposits that may affect an injector's spray or flow when the injector design and findings support that approach. The suitable cleaning method depends on the fuel system. Cleaning is not equivalent to injector replacement and cannot correct every cause of rough running or poor fuel delivery.

Learn More 08.04

Fuel System Diagnostics

Fuel system diagnostics evaluates whether fuel reaches the engine in the correct quantity and at the required pressure. It can investigate starting, drivability, fuel-odor, and delivery concerns. The approach depends on whether the vehicle uses gasoline or diesel and on its injection arrangement.

Learn More 14.05

Propane Injector Replacement

Propane injector replacement addresses a confirmed fault in an LPG injector or applicable injector assembly. These components meter propane according to the installed fuel system's design. They are not interchangeable with gasoline injectors, and the repair must account for system compatibility, sealing, and any specified setup requirements.
