Wheel Removal

Wheel removal takes a wheel-and-tire assembly off the vehicle to allow inspection, seasonal changes, or access to other components. It is not the same as dismounting a tire from its wheel. Safe lifting, support, and careful handling of the mounting hardware are central to the service.

D&S / SERVICE DEPT.15.02

Why removal may be needed

  • Brakes, suspension, tires, or wheel mounting surfaces require access.
  • A wheel set is being changed or a damaged assembly needs assessment.

Conditions can affect the scope

Corrosion can seize a wheel to its hub, and damaged fasteners or locking-wheel hardware may complicate removal. Forcing an unsuitable method can damage the wheel, studs, or surrounding components. Inspection should identify these issues and determine a controlled approach rather than treating every wheel as freely removable.

What the visit can include

The vehicle is supported appropriately, and the wheel and hardware are removed using suitable procedures. Mounting surfaces and fasteners can be assessed once accessible. Any subsequent installation is a separate step that requires the correct hardware, tightening sequence, and torque. If another repair is planned, that work should be identified clearly on the service request.

What to bring

Provide the wheel-lock key if fitted and mention aftermarket hardware or spacers. Tell D&S whether the wheel is being removed for a tire concern, a seasonal change, or another repair. Do not attempt roadside removal in an unsafe traffic location; arrange assistance when safe access is not available.

Wheel Installation

Wheel installation fits a wheel-and-tire assembly to the vehicle using the correct mounting arrangement and fasteners. It is distinct from mounting a loose tire onto a wheel. Proper fit, clean contact surfaces, and the specified tightening procedure are important to secure attachment and reliable operation.

Learn More 15.04

Seasonal Tire Changeover

Seasonal tire changeover prepares the vehicle for a different tire or wheel set suited to the coming conditions. The work may involve swapping complete mounted assemblies or changing tires on the same wheels. Those are different operations, so the requested scope should be established before the appointment.

Learn More 15.05

Tire Repair

Tire repair begins with determining whether a damaged tire is eligible for a proper repair. Location, injury size, internal condition, and driving after pressure loss all matter. Not every puncture can be repaired, and an external appearance check alone cannot establish that the tire is sound inside.
