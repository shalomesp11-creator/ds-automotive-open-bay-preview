Tire & Wheel Services

Tire and wheel services address mounting the wheel assemblies, maintaining appropriate pressure, balancing rotating mass, and assessing repairable tire damage. Wheel balancing is different from wheel alignment, and neither automatically repairs a damaged tire or loose suspension component.

D&S / SERVICE DEPT.15

Reasons to arrange a visit

  • Vibration appears at certain road speeds or follows tire work.
  • Seasonal wheel or tire changes are due.
  • A tire loses pressure, has a puncture concern, or needs a pressure check.

Fit and inspection matter

Wheel dimensions, load requirements, fasteners, tire condition, and pressure-monitoring equipment must suit the vehicle. A tire that repeatedly needs air requires leak investigation rather than treating inflation as a permanent repair.

Damage can make driving unsafe

A bulge, exposed cord, rapid pressure loss, or severe vibration warrants stopping safely and arranging assistance. The services below explain installation, removal, balancing, seasonal changeovers, repair assessment, and pressure checks without assuming every puncture can be repaired.

Services in this category

15.01

Wheel Installation

Wheel installation fits a wheel-and-tire assembly to the vehicle using the correct mounting arrangement and fasteners. It is distinct from mounting a loose tire onto a wheel. Proper fit, clean contact surfaces, and the specified tightening procedure are important to secure attachment and reliable operation.

Learn More 15.02

Wheel Removal

Wheel removal takes a wheel-and-tire assembly off the vehicle to allow inspection, seasonal changes, or access to other components. It is not the same as dismounting a tire from its wheel. Safe lifting, support, and careful handling of the mounting hardware are central to the service.

Learn More 15.03

Tire and Wheel Balancing

Tire and wheel balancing corrects uneven mass distribution in a rotating assembly. Appropriate weights help reduce vibration caused by imbalance. Balancing is different from alignment, which concerns wheel geometry, and it does not repair a bent wheel, damaged tire, or worn suspension joint.

Learn More 15.04

Seasonal Tire Changeover

Seasonal tire changeover prepares the vehicle for a different tire or wheel set suited to the coming conditions. The work may involve swapping complete mounted assemblies or changing tires on the same wheels. Those are different operations, so the requested scope should be established before the appointment.

Learn More 15.05

Tire Repair

Tire repair begins with determining whether a damaged tire is eligible for a proper repair. Location, injury size, internal condition, and driving after pressure loss all matter. Not every puncture can be repaired, and an external appearance check alone cannot establish that the tire is sound inside.

Learn More 15.06

Tire Pressure Check

A tire pressure check compares inflation with the vehicle's specified pressure for its tires and loading conditions. The vehicle placard or applicable manufacturer information is the starting point, not the maximum pressure molded into the tire sidewall. Front and rear requirements may differ.
