Built by Family.

Working with my sons allows me to pass on more than technical knowledge. It allows me to share the values that have shaped my entire career.

The Journey Continues

My journey began in Ukraine in 1992. Today, it continues in America, where my sons and I are building the next chapter of our family business.

My eldest son, David, continues our legacy in Ukraine, while we establish and grow D&S Automotive Repair Solutions here in the United States.

Different countries. Different chapters. The same commitment to quality.

To me, the American dream is not simply about starting a business in a new country. It is about earning trust, creating opportunities for the next generation, and continuing to do work you believe in.

More than 30 years of experience. One family. A lifetime dedicated to automotive excellence.
